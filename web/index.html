FirstWords: Language Screen

Screen a child's language from a transcript, typed sentences, or a recording. Everything runs on this device; nothing is uploaded. A screening aid, not a diagnosis.

Drop a file
Drag a .cha transcript or an audio file here, or tap to choose one.
CHILDES transcripts (.cha) read the child's age from the file. Audio is transcribed on this device.
Type the child's sentences, one per line
Use the child's exact words, one utterance per line. 10 or more utterances works best.
Record the child talking
Ask the child to tell a story or describe a picture. Short pauses between sentences help.
No child here? Read a demo script aloud

The screen measures what is said (sentence length and vocabulary), not the voice, so an adult can read a child-style script. Set the age to 6 years 0 months, tap Record, read one sentence at a time with a short pause after each, then Stop and Analyze.

Script A: on track for age 6
  1. The boy had a pet frog.
  2. At night the frog got out.
  3. The jar was empty in the morning.
  4. He looked inside his boots.
  5. The dog got stuck in the jar.
  6. They went outside to find it.
  7. An owl flew out of the tree.
  8. A deer carried the boy away.
  9. They fell into the pond.
  10. Then they found the frog family.
Script B: short, simple sentences (screens as a delay)
  1. The boy has a frog.
  2. The frog is in a jar.
  3. The boy is sleeping.
  4. The frog gets out.
  5. The boy looks.
  6. He looks in the boot.
  7. The dog looks too.
  8. The dog falls down.
  9. They go outside.
  10. They look and look.
  11. They see the frog.
  12. The boy is happy.

Prototype for research and science-fair demonstration. Words-only screen (sentence length, vocabulary diversity, age) trained on public CHILDES transcripts; on 345 children it never saw it scored AUC 0.86 and caught about 9 in 10 children with a language disorder. The delay flag is validated for ages 4 to 10 only, and recordings are not yet validated on real child speech. Not a diagnosis: talk to a speech-language pathologist about any concern.
